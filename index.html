Control & dynamics · Research

Accepted to IFAC 2026

Quadrotor Trajectory Tracking

Extended a prior SO(3) sliding-mode controller for multirotors from asymptotic to finite-time convergence of position and attitude tracking errors.

Contribution
Derived the control laws and stability analysis, built the MATLAB/Simulink simulation, and wrote the paper as first author. Also ran the controller in PX4 software-in-the-loop simulation with Gazebo and ROS 2.
Evidence
Figure-eight tracking in nominal simulation · finite-time convergence of position and attitude errors · trajectory and error plots from the paper
